Core insights and methodology

Strategic

The MASA dashboard integrates hazard, exposure, and socio-economic data to assess flood risk across Malaysia, Indonesia, and the Philippines. It focuses on flood frequency, severity, and mitigation impact with a practical decision-support view.

Region

Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines

Architecture

Hazard + Exposure + Impact

Key drivers

Rainfall, urban growth, vulnerability

Sources

World Bank, WHO, EM-DAT

Insights at a glance

  • Rainfall intensity remains the strongest driver of flood risk.
  • Indonesia shows the highest exposure and event frequency.
  • The Philippines is vulnerable to high-severity, low-frequency events.
  • Malaysia's urban exposure is rising rapidly.
  • Resilience measures can lower expected losses 20–30%.
